Dual-Modality Photoacoustic and White-Light Endoscopy

Nov 2025 – Present

Research Assistant, Prof. Lei Xi's Group, SUSTech

  • Independently designed the white-light fusion optical module, enabling simultaneous, co-registered, and co-field-of-view acquisition of photoacoustic and white-light signals (probe diameter: 8 mm), overcoming the temporal multiplexing limitation of conventional dual-modality systems.
  • Completed mechanical housing design and assembly using SolidWorks; established a non-invasive in vivo dual-modality endoscopic imaging protocol for rabbit vaginal and uterine tissues, including disease modeling and longitudinal tracking.
  • Outcome: Manuscript in preparation, expected submission to Photoacoustics (Nov 2026). Co-first author (2nd position), responsible for all experimental design, data acquisition, and analysis for the rabbit model.

Tri-Modality Imaging System for Stroke Pathophysiology

Mar 2025 – Jul 2025

Principal Investigator, Undergraduate Climbing Program, SUSTech

  • Independently performed mouse cranial window surgery and conducted longitudinal in vivo imaging of photothrombotic stroke models (single session: 2 hours continuous imaging; timepoints: Days 1, 4, 7, and 14 post-stroke).
  • Developed a feature-based image registration and correction algorithm in MATLAB, achieving high-precision spatial alignment and cross-temporal quantitative analysis across photoacoustic, fluorescence, and speckle modalities.
  • Key findings: Real-time observation of spreading depolarization waves post-stroke; revealed neurovascular decoupling during acute stroke, particularly in high-frequency components; tracked microvascular regeneration in the ischemic region using photoacoustic imaging.
