About

I am a final-year undergraduate student in Biomedical Engineering at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), where I work with Prof. Lei Xi on multi-modality biomedical imaging.

My research focuses on photoacoustic microscopy, optical system design, and in vivo preclinical imaging. I have experience developing image registration algorithms in MATLAB, building end-to-end imaging systems (optical path, data acquisition, and control software in LabVIEW), and conducting longitudinal animal studies, including mouse cranial window surgery and rabbit endoscopic imaging.

I am currently seeking graduate opportunities in biophotonics and biomedical imaging laboratories.
